I was tooling around with GIS a bit, despite not knowing GIS, to get a sense of land use changes in Utah in the last few decades. I have been thinking about the broad historical progression in the West from farmland to city. We could even back up a bit more, all the way to the 1820s, and think about forts and fur trading centers in their role as colonial outposts. But, as with mining towns, these did not tend to develop directly into the Western cities that we have today. So with this grand sweep of history in mind, I was curious what the progression of land use changes looks like when zoomed in.



Utah has a really nice online tool found [here](https://sgid-utah.opendata.arcgis.com/).
